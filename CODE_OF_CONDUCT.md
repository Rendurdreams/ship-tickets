# Ship Tickets Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and trade-offs.
- Respect privacy and never share another person's private information.
- Accept moderation decisions and corrections gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sustained bad-faith participation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing Ship Tickets.
